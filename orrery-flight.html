      How to play
      Goal
      • Destroy all enemy infrastructure to win; lose all of yours and you lose.
      • The enemy starts at Jupiter or Saturn, knows where your yard is, and expands toward you.
      Select & fleets
      • Click a ship or row · Shift-click adds · drag a box · CtrlA all
      • Shift+1–9 saves a group, 1–9 recalls it
      • + Fleet makes a fleet. Its header: click selects, ▾ folds it, + adds, − removes, ✕ disbands
      Orders
      • Right-click a planet or moon: orbit it · empty space or M: move there (Shift + mouse sets height)
      • Right-click one of your ships: track it · left-click a planet: trip time
      • Ships ordered together fly as a formation: arrange it in the side panel, double-click a ship there to make it leader
      Economy & towing
      • Select a shipyard to build: base metals for structure, noble metals for weapons, sensors, reactors
      • Ships near your infrastructure refuel, rearm and repair from it
      • Collectors: pick a resource in their panel, or Pick source (Alt + right-click) a body or field. Deeper gravity wells give less
      • Towing: select a station, right-click where it should go. The nearest free ship comes, hooks it and tows it. Add tug for more thrust, Release to let go (or select ships and the station, then give an order)
      Combat
      • Right-click an enemy: target · Shift: pursue · box several enemies, then right-click one: target the group
      • G auto / manual fire (Z X C) · E evasive · V vision rings · Y shipyard
      • Armour only shields hits on its own side (nose, flanks, tail). Fields shrink your sensors
      Camera & keys
      • Right-drag rotate · scroll zoom · WASD pan · F follow · double-click focus · Space sensors
      • P pause · , . warp · T auto-warp · H hide controls · ? help · Esc cancel
      • Arrows and PgUp PgDn hand-fly the selection
